# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Pixon to be a friendly, welcoming project for everyone who contributes or uses it, whatever
their background, experience, age, identity, ability or opinions.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient. Assume good intent and ask questions before drawing conclusions.
- Give and accept feedback on the work, not the person.
- Respect different skill levels and different ways of working.
- Keep discussion on topic and useful to the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats or discriminatory language or jokes
- Personal attacks, trolling or deliberately derailing discussions
- Publishing someone's private information, including their photos, without permission
- Unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that break this code,
and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behavior they judge harmful to the community.

To report a problem, contact a project maintainer privately (through the contact details on their
GitHub profile). Reports are handled confidentially, and maintainers will not retaliate against anyone
who reports in good faith.

This code applies in all project spaces: the repository, issues, pull requests and any other forum
where people represent the project.

This text is in the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
